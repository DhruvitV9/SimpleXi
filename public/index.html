Stocks

Scores are algorithmic screening output for research, not trade recommendations, and do not guarantee any outcome. Gap is measured from previous close to the indicative pre-open price. Open interest change and buildup describe the previous session, since pre-market open interest is not fabricated; missing fields show N/A and are left out of the score rather than guessed (the Upstox feed gives no pre-open volume history, so Vol ratio is N/A there). The holiday calendar is not loaded, so the market phase shown assumes every weekday is a trading day.

Scoring settings

Weights are relative: each score is rescaled to 0 to 100 over the components that have data, so they do not need to add up to 100. Changes apply immediately and are saved in this browser.
